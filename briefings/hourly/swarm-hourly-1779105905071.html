Macro Context: The current market pulse exhibits a stagnant hourly trend, with no discernible strategic drift. However, this observation must be tempered by the looming structural fragility of the 2026-2027 horizon.

Gamma Squeeze: The recent surge in gamma-driven trading has created a precarious situation, with many market participants holding large gamma positions. As the market becomes increasingly sensitive to volatility, a gamma squeeze is likely to occur, leading to a sharp correction in market prices.

Institutional Implications: The analysis suggests that institutional investors should adopt a defensive strategy, focusing on high-quality bonds and dividend-paying stocks. A reduction in market exposure is also recommended, given the high probability of a market correction.
