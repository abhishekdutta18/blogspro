Regulatory friction is expected to increase in the 2026-2027 horizon, driven by:


1. ESG: Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) regulations are expected to tighten, leading to increased costs for companies with poor ESG ratings.
2. Taxation: Taxation policies are expected to become more stringent, leading to increased costs for companies with high tax liabilities.
3. Antitrust: Antitrust regulations are expected to become more aggressive, leading to increased scrutiny of large corporations.


Liquidity Friction


Liquidity friction is expected to increase in the 2026-2027 horizon, driven by:


1. High-Frequency Trading: High-frequency trading (HFT) algorithms are expected to become more prevalent, leading to increased market volatility.
2. Dark Pools: Dark pools are expected to become more popular, leading to increased market fragmentation.
3. Market Makers: Market makers are expected to become more selective, leading to increased bid-ask spreads.
