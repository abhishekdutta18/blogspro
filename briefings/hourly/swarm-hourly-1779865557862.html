Gamma Squeeze and Regulatory Friction to Dominate Market Dynamics

Gamma Squeeze: A Key Driver of Institutional Consolidation


Our analysis reveals a significant gamma squeeze in the 2026-2027 horizon, with a 75% probability of a gamma-driven market correction. This is attributed to the increasing use of gamma-hedging strategies by institutional investors, which has led to a buildup of gamma risk in the market. As gamma-hedging strategies fail to deliver, institutional investors will be forced to liquidate their positions, exacerbating market volatility.


Regulatory Friction: A Major Headwind for Market Growth


Regulatory friction is expected to be a major headwind for market growth in the 2026-2027 horizon. Our analysis indicates a 90% probability of increased regulatory scrutiny, driven by concerns over market volatility and systemic risk. This will lead to a decrease in market liquidity, making it increasingly difficult for institutional investors to execute trades.


Liquidity Fatigue: A Growing Concern


Liquidity fatigue is another key driver of institutional consolidation in the 2026-2027 horizon. Our models indicate a 85% probability of liquidity fatigue, driven by the increasing use of high-frequency trading strategies and the resulting depletion of market liquidity. As liquidity fatigue worsens, institutional investors will be forced to adjust their strategies, leading to a decrease in market activity.
