This divergence is the primary indicator of an imminent structural deleveraging cycle.

This suggests that the banking sector’s plumbing is operating at maximum capacity, with minimal elasticity to absorb systemic shocks. If collateral haircuts move by a mere 50 basis points, the subsequent margin calls will trigger a cascade of forced liquidations, hitting the tech-heavy equity stack first.

Once current net-long gamma positions expire, the lack of institutional buyers will lead to a rapid evaporation of the bid-side liquidity, pushing the market into a vacuum-driven draw-down.
