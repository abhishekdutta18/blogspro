Tail Risk Assessment


Regulatory headwinds, liquidity friction, and market sentiment are expected to dominate market dynamics in the 2026-2027 horizon. Key tail risks include:


1. Regulatory Friction: Increased scrutiny of financial institutions, leading to reduced lending and investment activity.
2. Liquidity Friction: Reduced market liquidity, exacerbating price volatility and increasing the risk of flash crashes.
3. Market Sentiment: Shifts in investor sentiment, driven by changes in economic fundamentals, geopolitical events, or unexpected policy decisions.


Structural Fragility


The 2026-2027 horizon is expected to be characterized by extreme structural fragility, driven by:


1. Debt Overhang: Elevated levels of corporate and government debt, increasing the risk of default and credit contagion.
2. Monetary Policy Normalization: Gradual reduction in monetary policy accommodation, leading to higher interest rates and reduced liquidity.
3. Geopolitical Uncertainty: Increased tensions between major powers, driving market volatility and reducing investor confidence.


Institutional Consolidation
