By decoupling domestic capital from traditional shadow-lending, the regulator has effectively created an "observability tax" on all market participants. Institutional entities clinging to 2025-era expansion narratives are ignoring the delta-drift: capital is being funneled into a bottleneck. The resulting friction is not a temporary market condition; it is the new structural reality. When systemic liquidity is trapped within high-observability payment rails, the capacity for large-scale derivative hedging evaporates. 2. THE CONVERTIBLE BOND FALLACY
The AI-linked convertible bond issuance seen through late 2025 was a high-leverage desperation move. These instruments are now failing the collateral purity test. As we approach Q3 2026, the credit risk premiums associated with "AI-adjacent" corporate debt are showing signs of terminal widening. The market is attempting to price in a "Google Effect"—the delusion that algorithmic efficiency can perpetually subsidize interest-servicing costs—but the math has finally turned. Institutional liquidators are advised to treat these bonds as toxic assets. Any remaining "bullish" deltas in these sectors are merely ghost-signals from retail algorithms triggered by localized short-covering cycles.


3. REGULATORY FRICTION AS A SYSTEMIC RESTRAINT
SEBI’s aggressive, heavy-handed containment of retail gamma isn't protecting the market; it is removing the pressure-relief valve. By suppressing retail-led speculative volatility, the regulator has engineered a market that lacks the necessary counter-party depth to withstand a major external shock. We have moved from a market that "breathes" (volatility as discovery) to a market that "stiffens" (volatility as potential for cascade). The lack of retail participants means that institutional liquidity exits will be met with a vacuum, not a bid.

      • Cross-Asset Correlation Reality: 2025 saw a decoupling of tech and physical commodity assets. That decoupling is now reverting to a brutal, high-correlation collapse. When the liquidity vacuum hits, everything—AI-tech, digital payment providers, and industrial debt—will re-price in the same direction: downward.

      • Sovereign Paper: Maintain max allocation in short-duration IG sovereign debt. It is the only reliable buffer against a systemic credit freeze.

      • Physical Gold: The only viable insurance against the ECB-RBI liquidity retreat. Avoid gold-backed synthetic derivatives; they are subject to the same collateral decay as the underlying market.

The "Bullish" case (P=0.10) is a statistical vanity metric—an outlier probability based on the assumption that AI financing velocity is infinite. It is not.
