The "Regulatory Contagion" thesis identified by the Coding Architect is the primary vector for the impending liquidity freeze. The retail delta is being squeezed out, leaving institutions holding an bag of illiquid, high-latency silicon that will suffer a 40%+ valuation write-down upon the next audit cycle.

Expect a "Liquidity Void" in data center junk debt, where the bid-ask spread effectively becomes infinite.
